Patient GuidePatient education · General information

What to know

A little preparation can make a specialist appointment more organized. Bring previous medical records and a clear description of your symptoms so the clinician can review relevant information efficiently.

Common points to discuss with a doctor

  • Previous medical reports and scan results
  • Current prescriptions and medication details
  • A list of symptoms and when they started
  • Previous treatment records
  • Questions you want to ask during the consultation
Medical note

Your consultation time and any investigations depend on your individual clinical situation.
