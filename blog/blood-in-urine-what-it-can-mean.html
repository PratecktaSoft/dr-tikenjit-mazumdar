Urology GuidePatient education · General information

What to know

Blood in urine is called hematuria. It can be visible or detected only on a urine test. Causes range from infection and stones to prostate or urinary-tract conditions, so it should not be self-diagnosed.

Common points to discuss with a doctor

  • Visible pink, red or brown urine
  • Blood found on urinalysis
  • Burning or urinary symptoms
  • Back, side or bladder discomfort
Medical note

Blood in the urine can have many causes. A clinician can determine which tests are appropriate.
