Urinary Tract Imaging: What Patients Should Know

Diagnostic UrologyPatient education · General information

What to know

Imaging tests can help clinicians evaluate the kidneys, ureters and bladder. Depending on the clinical question, imaging may include ultrasound, CT, MRI or other techniques.

Common points to discuss with a doctor

  • Persistent urinary symptoms
  • Blood in urine
  • Recurrent urinary infections
  • Abdominal, back or groin pain
  • Suspected obstruction or stones
Medical note

The appropriate imaging test depends on the symptoms, examination and clinical question.
