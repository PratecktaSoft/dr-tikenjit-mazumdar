Urology GuidePatient education · General information

What to know

A urology consultation may be appropriate when urinary or reproductive-system symptoms persist, recur, or need specialist assessment. A urologist can evaluate a wide range of conditions affecting the kidneys, ureters, bladder, prostate and related structures.

Common points to discuss with a doctor

  • Persistent changes in urination
  • Blood in the urine
  • Recurrent urinary infections
  • Kidney or urinary stones
  • Difficulty emptying the bladder
  • Symptoms that require specialist evaluation
Medical note

Individual symptoms can have several possible causes. This page is general education and does not diagnose a condition.
